Ai Room — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

Ai Room is a personal project by Tilak Thapa. When you sign in with Google, Ai Room receives your name, email address, and profile photo, only to identify your account.

API keys you enter in Settings are stored only for your account on the server running Ai Room, are never shown back to you or anyone else, and can be removed anytime by clearing the field.

Questions you ask are sent to the AI providers you enabled (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, or OpenRouter) and are handled under those providers’ own policies; answers and transcripts are stored only for your account.

Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or trackers.
