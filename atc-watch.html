About this page

ATC Watch is an unofficial student project. It reads only the FAA's public status feeds — no login, no credentials, nothing is sent to FAA or airline systems, and it is not affiliated with the FAA. A delay pattern here is a prompt to look, not a safety verdict: three delayed airports can happen on a busy weather day, and a clean verdict only means nothing in the public feeds looks wrong. Source code: github.com/Tilakthapa3/atc-watch.
